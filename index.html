    Get your vision score. 20/20 is the goal.

    The doodle is built from ovals, rectangles, triangles, lines, arcs and dots on a 20 × 20 grid. The grader finds every part in your strokes and checks its shape (40%), size (25%), where it sits (20%) and which way it turns (15%). Big, signature parts count most, and stray ink costs points. One scored exam a day, the same for everyone. Endless and Party are unlimited.
