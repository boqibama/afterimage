Glance

Ten seconds to look. A minute to draw it back.

 

How to play

  1. Press Start and study the picture for 10 seconds: a small doodle (a pig, a penguin in a hat, a cat on a bicycle) built from ovals, rectangles, triangles, half-ovals, lines, arcs and dots on a 20 × 20 grid.
  2. It vanishes. Draw it back from memory on the same grid with your mouse, trackpad or finger, in as many strokes as you like. The eraser and Undo fix mistakes.
  3. The grader finds each object and each of its parts in your strokes and checks every part on four things, most important first: its shape (40%), its size (25%), where it sits (20%) and which way it turns (15%). Big and signature parts count for more than small details. Ink that draws nothing in the picture costs points.
  4. One scored picture a day, the same for everyone. Practice is unlimited. The Scorecard tab shows every measurement behind your score.
